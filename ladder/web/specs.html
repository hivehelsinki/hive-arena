Ratings

Ratings follow OpenSkill's Plackett-Luce model: each team has a skill estimate μ and an uncertainty σ, updated after every match from the places of all its players (ties included). Teams are ranked by μ − 3σ, so a new team starts at the bottom.

Results are kept forever. Replays and bot outputs are kept for each team's last {{.KeepMatchFiles}} matches.

Running a match

Containersa fresh game server and one container per bot, on a private network without internet access
Resourcesper bot: {{.Config.Runner.CPUPerBot}} CPU core, {{.Config.Runner.Memory}} of memory, {{.Config.Runner.Pids}} processes
