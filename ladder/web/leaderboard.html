Format

  • The latest tag on the main branch of each team's repository plays, from its first match after it is built (new tags are checked every few minutes).
  • Matches are played one at a time, all day. Each team plays {{.Settings.Quota}} matches per 24 hours in each format: {{range $i, $f := .Ladder.Formats}}{{if $i}}, {{end}}{{$f.Map}} ({{$f.Players}} players){{end}}.
  • Matches are challenges: a team challenges teams drawn among the {{.Settings.Window}} ranked just above it in that format ({{percent .Settings.RandomShare}} of the matches are against random teams instead). Seats are random.
  • Ratings move after every match, more for teams with few matches (a new team starts at 0). A new version makes its team's rating more uncertain again, so that it can move quickly.
  • Bots are ranked by flowers; a bot that crashes or doesn't join is last. Points per match go from 60 (first) to 0 (last), equal flowers share the points of the places they span.
